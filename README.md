# PATHFINDER-SUPERMARKET-DASHBOARD-ANALYSIS-
### Overview 
This is a well-structured Power BI dashboard tracking the performance of Pathfinder Supermarket across regions and product categories.

### Key Insights
1. Healthy Overall Profitability
  A Net Profit Margin of 24.2% (₦1.24M profit on ₦5.1M revenue) is strong for a supermarket. This indicates good cost control and a healthy product mix.

2. Electronics remains the revenue leader
  It contributes the highest revenue (₦1.83M) and the highest volume (7,109 units). However, we cannot yet see if it also delivers the highest profit contribution.

3. Clothing is a strong secondary driver
  High volume + solid revenue makes it a reliable performer.

4. Beauty is the weakest category by both revenue and volume
  Lowest sales (₦766K) and lowest units (2,995). This category needs attention.

5. Regional performance is fairly balanced
  West leads in quantity and revenue, while East and South are slightly behind. The differences are not extreme, which is positive.

6. Strong Average Metrics
  An AOV of ₦1,022 and average unit price of ₦253 show customers are buying reasonably sized baskets with a good mix of higher-value items.

### Recommendations
1. Protect and grow the 24.2% margin
  - Monitor COGS closely, especially in Electronics (often lower margin).
  - Avoid heavy discounting that could erode the current healthy margin.

3. Boost Beauty category
  - Review pricing, assortment, placement, and promotions.
  - Beauty often carries higher margins — improving its volume could significantly lift overall profit.

4. Regional strategy
  - West: Maintain and expand (current leader).
  - East & South: Investigate lower performance — check product availability, local preferences, or competition.
